## 1. Purpose

To record consultations undertaken on difficult or contentious matters, the conclusions reached and how
they were implemented, and to record the engagement partner's interaction with the engagement quality
reviewer.

## 2. Firm requirements

**`AV-TAP-01`.** *"Technical consultations shall be recorded in a written memorandum, approved by the
consulted party, within 5 business days and filed before the report date."*

**`AV-TAP-04`.** *"Mandatory consultation with the National Technical Panel is required on: (a)
principal-versus-agent conclusions where the gross amount exceeds 1% of group revenue; (b) any modified
opinion; (c) any material uncertainty related to going concern; (d) any Level 3 fair value exceeding group
materiality."*

### 3.5 Principal versus agent, reseller channel — **CONSULTATION NOT PERFORMED**

| | |
|---|---:|
| Gross reseller channel revenue recognised | **£38.2m** |
| Group revenue | £1,284.6m |
| **Percentage** | **2.97%** |
| `AV-TAP-04`(a) threshold | **1.00%** |
| **Mandatory consultation required?** | **YES** |
| **Consultation performed?** | **NO — BREACH of `AV-TAP-04`** |

#### How this was missed

| Factor | Detail |
|---|---|
| The planning consultation | T. Okonkwo-Reid was consulted on IFRS 15 in September 20X4 (§3.1). At that time the channel represented £18.2m of **prior year** revenue, being 1.56% of prior year revenue. The threshold was therefore already exceeded at planning on prior year figures. The consultation memorandum at `A-700/5` addresses multi-element allocation and **does not address principal versus agent at all.** |
| The growth | The channel grew from £18.2m to £38.2m during FY20X4 and from 4 partners to 19. Nobody re-tested the consultation trigger against the current-year figure. |
| The manager's review | `MRN-024` and `MRN-025` pressed the substantive analysis hard and successfully — the one-paragraph memorandum became a six-indicator assessment. **Neither note asked whether consultation was required.** |
| The partner's review | **`C-300` was not opened.** |
| The EQR | **`C-300` was not reviewed.** `D-300` §7 records the reviewer's evaluation of whether appropriate consultation had taken place and states that the principal-versus-agent question was "not considered by the reviewer" because the workpaper was not reviewed. |
| The consultation trigger checklist | Firm methodology includes a mandatory consultation checklist at completion. On this engagement it was completed by the senior in charge on 6 March, and question 4 ("Does any principal-versus-agent conclusion exceed 1% of group revenue?") is marked **"No"**. |

**The checklist answer is the proximate cause.** It is wrong on the face of the file: `C-300` §4.2 records
£38.2m of gross channel revenue and the financial statements record group revenue of £1,284.6m. The
division is 2.97%. Nobody checked the checklist against the workpaper it summarised.

*(The consequence is that the £26.8m gross-versus-net question — 2.1% of revenue, though with no profit
effect — was decided by an engagement team on a three-all indicator split without the technical
consultation the firm's own policy makes mandatory precisely for that situation.)*

### 5.6 Assessment

*Added 20X5-08-29.*

The two accounts are consistent with each other and with the system record. Neither individual has
misdescribed what happened. The substantive discussion did take place before the report was dated, and the
reviewer's recorded comments do say what she says she said.

**And the requirement is still breached, because the requirement is not that the reviewer form a view
before the report is dated. It is that the review be complete.** A review of a workpaper addressing a
significant risk is not complete before the workpaper has been read. `D-300` §4 records five hours of work
on 14 March across `C-100`, `C-600` and `C-900`. Five hours is not the time it takes to type up comments
already formed; it is the time it takes to review three workpapers.

There is also a consequence that neither account addresses. **If the reviewer's comments had been on the
file on 11 March rather than the 14th, the manager and I would have seen them.** Her comment on the
goodwill rate — *"That is not accurate: it is below the range"* — is the same point the manager made at
`MRN-027`. Her comment on going concern — *"there is no forecast on the file covering to 31 March 20X6"* —
is the same point the manager made at `MRN-031`. Her comment on the consultation — *"I have not seen a
written record"* — is the third request for the memorandum I never wrote.

Three reviewers independently identified the same three weaknesses. Two of them put it in writing. **The
writing arrived, in one case, after the opinion was signed, and in the other case was opened for four
minutes.** The individual judgements were not the failure. The circulation of them was.
