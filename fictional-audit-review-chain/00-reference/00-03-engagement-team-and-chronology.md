**The engagement was under-supervised at the top and over-weighted at the bottom.** Manager hours
were less than half the benchmark. Partner review hours were roughly a seventh of it. Assistant
hours exceeded the benchmark range. This distribution is the single most predictive fact in the
file, and it is visible from the firm's own time system without reading a single workpaper. It is
the starting point of the inspection team's analysis at `I-200` and the regulator's at `R-500`.

## 7. Time-pressure map

The four working days from 9 to 12 March 20X5 carry the weight of the entire engagement.

```
 Mon 9 Mar   Manager review substantially complete
             MRN-031 (going concern period) raised and cleared same day
             12 of 45 manager notes closed on this day

 Tue 10 Mar  Partner review — 7.5 of his 11.5 total review hours
             Technical Panel telephone consultation on going concern (no memo)
             15 of 19 partner notes raised and 11 closed on this day

 Wed 11 Mar  EQR signs sections A and D-200 (not C-100, C-600, C-900)
             Audit Committee meeting, 14:00
             Final 6 manager notes closed
             Draft financial statements finalised, 22:40

 Thu 12 Mar  Board meeting 09:00 — financial statements approved
             Audit report signed 11:20
             Results announced to the market 07:00 on 13 March

 Sat 14 Mar  EQR signs C-100, C-600, C-900   ← two days after the report date
```

Every one of the ten issues in the spine was either created, cleared, or missed inside that window,
with the exception of `ISS-10`, which happened in May.
