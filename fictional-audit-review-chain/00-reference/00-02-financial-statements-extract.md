>
> The engagement team identified the LTIP as a fraud risk factor at `A-500`. **This sensitivity
> analysis appears nowhere in the audit file.** It was first performed by the regulator at `R-500`.

> ### ⚠ Note the classification of the RCF
>
> The £142.0m drawn revolving credit facility matures **27 February 20X6**. At the balance sheet
> date of 31 December 20X4 that is **14 months away**, so it is correctly classified as a
> **non-current** liability.
>
> That correct classification is precisely why the maturity fell out of view. Management's going
> concern assessment ran 12 months from the *balance sheet date*, to 31 December 20X5 — and the
> facility matures two months after that window closes. Had the assessment run 12 months from the
> *approval date* of 12 March 20X5, as it should have, the maturity would have been inside the
> period. See `ISS-07` / `C-900`.

> ### ⚠ The analytic that should have driven the audit
>
> | Metric | FY20X4 | FY20X3 | Movement |
> |---|---:|---:|---:|
> | Revenue growth | +10.0% | | |
> | **Contract assets** | **£128.4m** | £89.7m | **+43.1%** |

>
> Revenue grew 10% while unbilled contract assets grew 43% and cash conversion fell by a third.
> That is the classic signature of revenue being recognised ahead of billing and cash on
> over-time contracts.
>
> The engagement team's preliminary analytical review at `A-400/5` **identified this movement** and
> recorded management's explanation: *"Timing of milestone billings on CN-4471 and two other rail
> contracts; expected to unwind in H1 20X5."* The explanation was accepted. It was not corroborated
> against the billing schedules, and no procedure was designed in response. The manager's review
> note `MRN-003` asked about it and was cleared the same day.

## 12. Aggregate potential misstatement identified at Levels 4 and 5

Not known to the engagement team, the manager or the partner. Reproduced here for orientation.

| Issue | Description | Potential PBT effect £m | Effect on adjusted EPS? |
|---|---|---:|---|
| `ISS-01` | CN-4471 estimated costs to complete | 3.5 | **Yes — 6.1p** |
| `ISS-02` | Cut-off — incremental over the £1.9m already listed | 5.1 | **Yes — 8.9p** |
| `ISS-05` | Contingent consideration understated | 4.4 | No — remeasurement is an adjusting item |
| `ISS-06` | Industrial Systems CGU impairment | 22.1 | No — impairment is an adjusting item |
| | **Aggregate** | **35.1** | |
| | **As % of reported PBT of £62.4m** | **56.3%** | |

Plus `ISS-03`: revenue overstated by up to **£26.8m** (2.1% of revenue) with no effect on profit.

The final column matters for understanding motive, and it cuts both ways. Only two of the four
issues would have moved the metric on which the CFO's LTIP vests — but those two are precisely the
two that would have moved it below the vesting threshold. The other two (`ISS-05` and `ISS-06`)
carry no adjusted-EPS consequence at all, and no covenant consequence either, since goodwill
impairment and fair-value remeasurement are both excluded from Adjusted EBITDA. Whatever explains
the treatment of those two, it is not the incentive scheme. The regulator makes this distinction at
`R-500` §4 and treats it as evidence that the file's problem was general insufficiency of scepticism
rather than a targeted response to one incentive.
