# 00-05 — Glossary and Standards Referenced

---

> ## Note on the standards cited in this repository
>
> The professional standards named below (ISAs, ISQMs, IFRSs, the IESBA Code) are **real**. They
> are cited because the exercise is only instructive if real requirements are applied to the
> fictional facts.
>
> **Paragraph-level citations are indicative.** They are written for narrative plausibility and
> have not been verified against the extant text of each standard, which is revised periodically.
> Where this repository says "ISA 540 requires X", treat that as a statement about the *general
> thrust* of the standard, not as a quotation. Anyone using this material for training,
> examination or reference should check the current text.
>
> Jurisdiction-specific requirements are attributed to fictional Meridian instruments (the Audit
> Oversight Act 20W8, the Meridian Companies Act 20W1, the MSE Listing Rules) so that nothing here
> can be mistaken for a statement about any real jurisdiction's law.

## 3. Ashcroft Vane LLP internal policies referenced

| Policy | Content |
|---|---|
| `AV-IND-02` | Non-audit fees for a listed audit client shall not exceed **40%** of audit fees in any year. |
| `AV-TAP-01` | Technical consultations shall be recorded in a written memorandum, approved by the consulted party, **within 5 business days** and filed before the report date. |
| `AV-TAP-04` | **Mandatory consultation** with the National Technical Panel on: principal-versus-agent conclusions where the gross amount exceeds 1% of group revenue; any modified opinion; any material uncertainty related to going concern; any Level 3 fair value exceeding group materiality. |
| `AV-AUD-11` | The engagement partner shall review all workpapers addressing a significant risk, all critical accounting judgements, and the completion memorandum. |
| `AV-AUD-14` | Review notes shall be cleared by the reviewer who raised them. **Self-clearance by the preparer is not permitted.** |
| `AV-AUD-19` | Audit files shall be assembled within **60 days** of the report date. Post-assembly modification requires documented approval by the engagement partner and the Head of Quality. |
| `AV-QM-03` | The annual inspection programme shall select engagements on a risk-weighted basis, with elevated-risk engagements represented **at least in proportion to their share of the portfolio**. |
| `AV-QM-07` | Grading changes between draft and final inspection reports shall be documented with a written rationale approved by the Head of Quality. |
| `AV-QM-09` | Inspectors shall not review engagements on which they have served **within the preceding five years**. |

Four of these nine policies were breached on this engagement: `AV-IND-02`, `AV-TAP-01`,
`AV-TAP-04`, `AV-AUD-11`, `AV-AUD-14`, `AV-AUD-19`. (That is six. The regulator makes the same
observation at `R-500` and notes that the firm's own compliance monitoring detected none of them.)

`AV-QM-03`, `AV-QM-07` and `AV-QM-09` were breached by the **inspection function itself** — which
is the substance of Level 5.

## 4. Glossary

| Term | Meaning as used here |
|---|---|
| **Adjusted EBITDA** | EBITDA before exceptional items; the covenant measure. |
| **Aggregation risk** | The risk that individually immaterial misstatements are material in combination. |
| **ARR** | Annual recurring revenue. The Kestrel earn-out metric. |
| **Assembly period** | The window after the report date within which the final audit file must be compiled. 60 days here. |
| **CGU** | Cash-generating unit. The lowest level at which goodwill is monitored for impairment. |
| **Clearly trivial** | The threshold below which misstatements need not be accumulated. £155k here. |
| **Component materiality** | Materiality set for a component of the group, necessarily below group materiality. |
| **Contract asset** | Revenue recognised but not yet billed. £128.4m here, up 43%. |
| **Contract liability** | Amounts billed ahead of performance. |
| **Contingent consideration** | Deferred purchase price payable on future performance; measured at fair value at acquisition. |
| **Control indicators** | The IFRS 15 factors distinguishing a principal from an agent. |
| **Cut-off** | Whether a transaction is recorded in the correct period. |
| **EQR / EQCR** | Engagement quality review(er). An independent partner-level review completed **before** the report is dated. |
| **ETC** | Estimate of costs to complete. The CN-4471 number that moved by £4.1m. |
| **Extrapolation / projection** | Scaling a misstatement found in a sample up to the population. Not done here. |
| **Factual misstatement** | A misstatement about which there is no doubt. |
| **Headroom** | The excess of recoverable amount over carrying amount. 2.1% for Industrial Systems. |
| **Input method** | Measuring progress by inputs consumed — here, costs incurred over total estimated costs. |
| **ISQM 1 monitoring** | The firm's obligation to monitor and remediate its own system of quality management. The subject of Level 5. |
| **Judgemental misstatement** | A difference arising from a judgement the auditor considers unreasonable. |
| **Key audit matter (KAM)** | A matter of most significance in the audit, reported in the auditor's report. |
| **Level 3 fair value** | A fair value using significant unobservable inputs. The £11.5m earn-out. |
| **Look-back / retrospective review** | Comparing prior estimates to actual outcomes to assess management bias. Required by ISA 540; absent here. |
| **LTIP** | Long-term incentive plan. The CFO's vests on adjusted EPS. |
| **Management override** | The presumed fraud risk that management can circumvent controls. |
| **Material uncertainty** | Where events cast significant doubt on going concern; requires disclosure and a dedicated report section. |
| **Monetary unit sampling (MUS)** | Value-weighted sampling. Used at `C-200`. |
| **Performance materiality** | Materiality reduced to allow for aggregation risk. £2.17m here (70%). |
| **Projected misstatement** | The population-level estimate derived from sample errors. £7.0m here. |
| **Reverse stress test** | Working backwards to find what would have to happen for the entity to fail. Not performed. |
| **Root cause analysis (RCA)** | Determining why a deficiency arose, as opposed to what it was. Both Levels 4 and 5 criticise the RCA here — Level 5 criticises Level 4's. |
| **Safeguard** | A measure reducing an identified threat to independence to an acceptable level. |
| **Self-review threat** | The threat that the auditor evaluates the output of their own firm's prior work. |
| **Significant risk** | A risk requiring special audit consideration. Nine identified here. |
| **Sufficient appropriate audit evidence** | Enough evidence, of the right quality. The recurring deficiency. |
| **Value in use** | The present value of a CGU's future cash flows. |
| **WACC** | Weighted average cost of capital; the discount rate. 9.1% used; 10.2–11.6% recommended. |

## 5. Grading scales used

### Ashcroft Vane LLP internal inspection

| Grade | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **1** | Compliant. No findings requiring action. |
| **2** | Compliant with limited improvements required. |
| **3** | Significant improvements required. Sufficient appropriate evidence not obtained in one or more material areas. |
| **4** | Non-compliant. The opinion was not supported. |

The ARG engagement was graded **3 in draft** and **2 in final**.

### FRAOA public grading

| Grade | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **A** | Good, with limited improvements required. |
| **B** | Limited improvements required. |
| **C** | Improvements required. |
| **D** | **Significant improvements required.** Sufficient appropriate audit evidence was not obtained in respect of one or more material balances or disclosures. |

The FRAOA graded the ARG engagement **D**.

### FRAOA firm-wide systems assessment

`Effective` · `Effective with improvements required` · `Improvements required` · **`Not effective`**

The FRAOA assessed Ashcroft Vane LLP's **ISQM 1 monitoring and remediation** as **Not effective**.
