## How to read this register

Each note records who raised it, when, on what workpaper, what was asked, what came back, who closed it
and on what basis. The **"Basis of clearance"** column is the one that matters: it distinguishes notes
closed because new evidence was obtained from notes closed because someone gave an explanation.

| Basis code | Meaning | Count |
|---|---|---:|
| **E** | New audit evidence obtained | 24 |
| **X** | Explanation from management or the preparer accepted; no new evidence | **19** |
| **W** | Wording or presentation changed only | 6 |

|---|---|---:|
