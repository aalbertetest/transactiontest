## Purpose

Firm methodology requires the engagement manager to provide written feedback to each team member on an
engagement of this size, distinguishing between what the individual did and what the engagement made
possible.

I am recording that distinction deliberately, because on this engagement it matters. Several things went
wrong in this file. **Almost none of them were caused by the person who prepared the workpaper.**

## 5. Y. Demirci — IT Audit, Assistant Manager

**74 hours against a budget of 90.**

### Feedback

`B-200` is a thorough piece of technical work. You did not stop at the sample: when 6 exceptions came up
in 25 access requests you ran the full population against the role definition matrix and produced the
finding that matters — 14 users with excess access, and specifically **two finance users who can create
and approve journals and amend vendor master data and release payments.** You quantified the shared
support account at 1,247 transactions including 34 general ledger journals. You established that the
privileged access log has never been reviewed. You went through all 47 unapproved changes individually
and identified the two that altered posting logic.

Every fact needed to conclude that the Aventine control environment was not reliable in H2 is in your
workpaper, established by you, quantified by you.

**And then §8.2 concludes that reliance on IT general controls remains appropriate for the full year.**

I want to separate two things. The evidence-gathering is strong. The conclusion does not follow from it,
and the reasoning offered — that the deficiencies concern administration rather than processing, and that
no misstatement has been identified as arising from them — inverts the burden. The question is not
whether we found a misstatement caused by the deficiency; it is whether the control environment gives us
a basis for relying on the system, and your own findings say it does not.

**I also have to record my part.** I did not open your workpaper. You gave me the conclusion verbally on
4 March and I accepted it. The engagement partner opened it for 20 minutes. Between us we gave 20 minutes
of review to the workpaper that concludes on the reliability of the entire second-half ledger, and we
left the person who found the problems to decide on his own what they meant.

For next year: where your evidence points one way and your conclusion goes the other, that is exactly
the situation the specialist consultation process exists for, and you should use it rather than resolving
it alone at the bottom of a workpaper on 3 March.

### Overall

**Meets expectations on execution; the conclusion requires discussion at your appraisal.** I have asked
that the discussion include your reporting line as well as mine, because the structural point — a
specialist producing a conclusion on a significant risk that nobody in the engagement team reviews — is
not something you can fix on your own.
