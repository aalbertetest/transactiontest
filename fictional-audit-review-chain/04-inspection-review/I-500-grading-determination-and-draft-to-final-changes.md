## 1. The grading framework

| Grade | Definition |
|---|---|
| 1 | Compliant. No findings requiring action. |
| **2** | **Compliant with limited improvements required.** |
| **3** | **Significant improvements required. Sufficient appropriate audit evidence was not obtained in one or more material areas, or there was a significant failure to comply with firm methodology.** |
| 4 | Non-compliant. The audit opinion was not supported. |

`AV-QM-06`: *"An engagement shall be graded 3 where the inspection concludes that sufficient appropriate
audit evidence was not obtained in respect of a material balance, class of transactions or disclosure, or
where a significant failure to comply with firm methodology is identified."*

`AV-QM-07`: *"Grading changes between draft and final inspection reports shall be documented with a written
rationale approved by the Head of Quality."*

## 4. The four reclassifications and their recorded rationales

`AV-QM-07` requires a written rationale for each grading change approved by the Head of Quality. The
following were recorded on 11 September and approved on 26 September.

### 4.1 `INSP-F03` — partner review — retained as a finding, wording softened

**Not a reclassification.** The finding was retained. Five sentences were removed and a mitigation paragraph
added. See `I-200` §6.3 for the two texts side by side.

Removed: *"did not comply with ISA 220 (Revised)"*; *"significant failure of engagement-level quality
management"*; *"principal basis for the overall grading of 3"*; *"each contains a deficiency identified by
this inspection"*; *"was provided, in writing, thirteen hours before signing... and did not read it."*

**Recorded rationale:** *"The finding is retained. The characterisation is moderated to reflect the mitigating
circumstances of the compressed timetable and concurrent commitments, and the engagement partner's
constructive engagement with the inspection."*

*(Every underlying number in `I-200` §2 appears unchanged in both versions. What was removed is the
conclusion drawn from them. The engagement partner did not contest this finding: `I-600` §7.)*

### 4.2 `INSP-F04` → `INSP-IP09` — manager review

**Recorded rationale:** *"The matters raised are not disputed. However, the manager's own contemporaneous
documentation identified each of them and escalated the most significant, and the resourcing constraint was
known to and unaddressed by the engagement partner. The substance is more properly reflected in `INSP-F03`
and in the firm-wide resourcing action at `I-700`. Recording a separate finding against the manager would
double-count a single failure of engagement management."*

**FRAOA assessment (`R-200` §6.1):** *"Properly reasoned and, in our view, correct. It is the exception among
the four."*

### 4.3 `INSP-F05` → `INSP-IP10` — CN-4471 contract estimate

**Recorded rationale:** *"The engagement team obtained an explanation attributable to three identified
workstreams, corroborated by the Contract Review Board's contemporaneous minute, and tested costs incurred
and the percentage-complete calculation. **A look-back analysis is present at `C-100/13`, though it is
high-level and does not address the specific contract.** The estimate remains within a range that a
reasonable auditor could accept."*

> **`C-100/13` was added to the file on 19 May 20X5 — sixty-eight days after the audit report was signed —
> without the authorisation `AV-AUD-19` requires. `C-100` §7 as archived on 12 May 20X5 recorded that the
> retrospective review had not been performed. A second unauthorised modification on the same day amended
> that narrative to cross-refer to the new schedule.**
>
> **The rationale for downgrading the most significant substantive finding in the report relies on evidence
> that did not exist when the opinion was signed.**
>
> FRAOA: `R-300` §6; finding `FRAOA-F13`.

### 4.4 `INSP-F06` → `INSP-IP11` — going concern assessment period

**Recorded rationale:** *"Leverage of 2.47× against a 3.00× limit; liquidity of £199.3m; investment grade
rating affirmed November 20X4. The engagement partner consulted the National Technical Panel, whose advice on
the period point was that it was presentational. **The indicative refinancing term sheet was received on
22 April 20X5**, confirming the engagement partner's commercial judgement."*

> **Two objections, both available to NQMG on 11 September 20X5.**
>
> The term sheet was received six weeks **after** the report date. It cannot bear on the sufficiency of
> evidence held on 12 March.
>
> The Panel's own director told this inspection on 18 July 20X5 — eight weeks before the reclassification —
> that he **would not have countersigned the conclusion** had he known the file contained no term sheet, no
> credit approval, no correspondence and only an oral representation. **That interview is in NQMG's own
> working papers at `I-100/12` and is quoted in NQMG's own draft finding.**
>
> ARG's FY20X5 accounts, published 3 March 20X6, include a **material uncertainty related to going concern**.
>
> FRAOA: findings `FRAOA-F07`, `FRAOA-F08`.

### 4.5 `INSP-F07` → `INSP-IP12` — downside case covenant breach

**Recorded rationale:** *"The breach arises in a downside scenario at a single test date, by 0.08×, and the
scenario did not eventuate. The Group remained compliant at every actual test date during 20X5."*

> **FRAOA (`R-400` `FRAOA-F07`):** *"The purpose of a downside case is to test what happens if the downside
> occurs. That it did not occur is not a reason for not having audited it. The financial statements asserted
> covenant compliance throughout the assessment period in the downside scenario; the auditor reported on that
> assertion without testing it; and the assertion was false on the face of management's own model."*

## 5. The recorded dissent

*`I-500/5`, dated 20X5-09-16, from R. Achebe-Kim, Senior Manager, NQMG, who performed 44 of the 141
inspection hours.*

> *"I have read the revised draft and I do not agree with it. I am recording that here because the protocol
> allows me to and because I think somebody should be able to see, later, that the point was made.*
>
> *My difficulty is not with any single reclassification. It is that we have moved from six findings to two
> without a single fact changing. I have re-read the draft and the final side by side. Every number is the
> same. The £3.5m is still 113% of materiality. The projection is still £7.0m. The covenant still breaches at
> 3.08×. The assessment period is still three months short of the requirement and the facility still matures
> inside the gap.*
>
> *What changed is the adjectives.*
>
> *On `INSP-F05` I would want to know when `C-100/13` was put on the file before we rely on it. It is not
> referenced in the March completion memorandum and `C-100` §7 as I read it in July said the retrospective
> review had not been performed. Either I misread it or something has been changed. **I raised this with
> Nathaniel on 12 September and was told the file is what it is and we review it as we find it.***
>
> *On `INSP-F06`, we interviewed Osunkoya. He told us he would not have countersigned. We are now citing his
> advice as support for downgrading the finding. I do not know how to reconcile those two things.*
>
> *On `INSP-F07`, we are saying that because the downside did not happen, not auditing the downside is an
> improvement point. If that is right, no downside case ever needs auditing.*
>
> *I also want to record that the meeting on 9 September was not minuted and that neither I nor Gustav was in
> it. We did the fieldwork. I have no way of knowing what was said.*
>
> *I am not alleging bad faith by anybody. I think Marcus is genuinely worried about proportionality and I
> think Nathaniel genuinely believes the look-back analysis answered his finding. **But we are the firm's
> monitoring function. If we can find six things and report two, then what we report is not information about
> the firm's quality. It is information about what the firm was prepared to write down.***"*
