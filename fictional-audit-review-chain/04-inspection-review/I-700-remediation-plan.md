## 1. Requirement

ISQM 1 requires the firm to design and implement remedial actions that address identified deficiencies and
their root causes, to evaluate whether those actions have been implemented and are effective, and to modify
them where they are not.

`AV-QM-08` requires each remedial action to have a named owner, a target date, and a defined evidence-of-
completion test.

### 4.1 The three "not considered" items

Each of the three matters marked "Not considered" above was capable of being identified from the inspection's
own findings.

**The General Counsel referral.** `INSP-IP04` records £2.4m paid to an entity controlled by a director's
brother-in-law, undisclosed in the related party note, where the director answered "No" to a direct question
on a signed questionnaire. Whether that engages Companies Act s.412, and whether the firm has any obligation
arising from it, is a question for the General Counsel and not for NQMG. **It was not referred.**
FRAOA finding `FRAOA-F12`.

**The Audit Committee.** `I-200` §3.4 records that the engagement partner told the Audit Committee Chair in
private session that no matter concerning the CFO had been encountered, and that the engagement team held
information to the contrary. NQMG's terms of reference preclude client contact (`I-100` §4.2), which
explains why NQMG did not inform the Committee. **It does not explain why the question of whether somebody
should was not put to anyone who could.** FRAOA finding `FRAOA-F12`.

**The opinion.** See §4 above.

that the firm's ISQM 1 monitoring and remediation is **not effective**.

## Postscript — what the FY20X5 audit found

Actions E6 and E7 required the FY20X5 engagement team to perform the two procedures omitted in FY20X4. Both
were performed. The results are recorded here because they bear on the grading decision at `I-500`.

| Action | Procedure | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **E6** | Contract-level retrospective review of prior year contract cost estimates | **CN-4471: cumulative adverse variance against the 31 December 20X4 estimate of £9.1m.** Contract margin restated from 22.4% to 11.4%. The FY20X4 estimate of total costs of £74.8m proved to be understated by 12.2%. |
| **E7** | Reassessment of the Industrial Systems CGU discount rate against the specialist's range | **Rate of 10.4% adopted, inside the specialist's FY20X4 range of 10.2%–11.6%. Impairment of £16.2m recognised at the FY20X5 interims and a further £4.8m at the full year — £21.0m in total, against the £22.1m the FY20X4 sensitivity analysis at `C-600` §6 showed would arise at 10.2%.** |

Further, ARG's FY20X5 financial statements published on 3 March 20X6 **include a material uncertainty related
to going concern**, and the MRA delay claim was settled on 14 November 20X5 at **£4.1m against ARG** — a claim
management had assessed as "without merit" and expected to resist in full.

**Each of the three matters downgraded from a finding to an improvement point at `I-500` was, within twelve
months, resolved against the position the downgrade relied upon.**
