### 5.5 Proposed adjustment

**Class 388 spares.** Six lines with a gross value of £2.8m relate to a train class withdrawn from
service by the operator in September 20X4. Management's matrix provides these at the over-36-month
rate of 48%, giving a provision of £1.34m and a carrying value of £1.46m.

The audit team's assessment is that these parts have no realistic prospect of sale other than as
scrap metal and reclaimed electronic components.

| | £m |
|---|---:|
| Gross carrying value | 2.80 |
| Estimated net realisable value (scrap and reclaim, based on the £41/tonne and component reclaim rates evidenced in three FY20X4 disposals) | 0.22 |
| **Required provision** | **2.58** |
| Provision recorded by management | 1.34 |
| **Proposed adjustment** | **1.24** |

**Discussion.** Presented to the Group Financial Controller on 26 February 20X5 with the supporting
disposal evidence and the operator's withdrawal notice. Management challenged the scrap value
assumption and produced an inquiry from a Latvian operator that had expressed interest in acquiring
Class 388 units. The audit team obtained the correspondence: the inquiry is dated August 20X3, was
not pursued, and relates to complete units rather than spares.

**Management accepted the adjustment in full on 27 February 20X5.** It is recorded as corrected
misstatement C1 at `D-100`.

### 5.6 Conclusion on provisioning

Following the £1.24m adjustment, the provision is £19.84m, an effective rate of 8.5% of gross
inventory and 39.9% of the slow-moving band (£16.44m provided against £41.2m gross, up from 36.9%).
Recorded conclusion: adequate.
