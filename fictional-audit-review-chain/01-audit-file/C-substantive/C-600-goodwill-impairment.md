### 4.1 Reliability of management's forecasting for this CGU

| | FY20X4 forecast made at 31 Dec 20X3 | FY20X4 actual | Variance |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Revenue | £318.4m | £304.1m | **(4.5%)** |
| Operating cash flow | £18.9m | £15.4m | **(18.5%)** |

**Management's forecasts for this CGU have historically been optimistic.** The FY20X5 forecast of
£16.1m pre-tax operating cash flow represents growth of 4.5% on the FY20X4 actual of £15.4m, after
a year in which the CGU delivered 18.5% below the forecast made twelve months earlier.

**Recorded assessment:** *"The prior year forecast was not achieved. Management attributes this to
the deferral of two industrial process contracts into FY20X5, which supports the FY20X5 forecast
being achievable. We have obtained the order book for the CGU at 31 December 20X4, which stands at
£214m against £198m a year earlier, and which includes the two deferred contracts. On that basis the
FY20X5 forecast is considered achievable."*

### 5.2 The firm's valuation specialist

Dr Helena Brasseur was engaged to provide an independent range for the post-tax discount rate for
each CGU. Scope letter at `C-600/5`; memorandum dated 24 February 20X5 at `C-600/6`.

The specialist's memorandum states:

> *"I have derived an independent range for the post-tax weighted average cost of capital applicable
> to each of the Group's cash-generating units, using a comparable company approach with
> unit-specific adjustments for size, geography and earnings volatility.*
>
> | CGU | My range | Management's rate |
> |---|---|---|
> | Rail Systems | 9.2% – 10.4% | 9.4% |
> | **Industrial Systems** | **10.2% – 11.6%** | **9.1%** |
> | Aftermarket EMEA | 8.8% – 9.8% | 9.0% |
> | Aftermarket Americas | 9.9% – 11.1% | 10.3% |
> | Software | 11.0% – 12.8% | 11.2% |
>
> *Management's rates fall within my ranges for four of the five CGUs.*
>
> ***For the Industrial Systems CGU, management's rate of 9.1% falls below the bottom of my range.***
> *The principal drivers of my higher range for this unit are (a) the volatility of its historical
> earnings, which has a standard deviation approximately 1.8 times that of the Group as a whole, and
> (b) its exposure to cyclical process-industry capital expenditure. Management's gearing assumption
> of 22% also appears low relative to the Group's actual gearing of 39% at the year end; a higher
> gearing assumption would reduce the WACC, but the effect is more than offset by the higher asset
> beta appropriate to this unit.*
>
> *I would be pleased to discuss the Industrial Systems CGU further if that would assist."*

### 5.3 Assessment recorded by the engagement team

> *"The specialist's range for the Industrial Systems CGU is 10.2% to 11.6% and management has used
> 9.1%. We have considered this difference.*
>
> *Management's rate is derived from a conventional WACC build-up using the Group's actual cost of
> borrowing, and includes a CGU-specific premium of 0.6% which demonstrates that management has
> considered unit-specific risk. The specialist's range is derived from comparable companies, which
> necessarily reflects the capital structures and risk profiles of other entities rather than ARG's.*
>
> *Management's rate of 9.1% is at the low end but is within a reasonable range given the Group's
> actual cost of borrowing. Acceptable."*

*(No further procedure was performed. The specialist's offer to discuss the CGU further was not
taken up. No consultation memorandum, note of a discussion, or revised specialist view is on the
file.)*

Performed by the engagement team using the independently rebuilt model.

| Discount rate | Value in use £m | Headroom / (impairment) £m |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 8.5% (management's WACC before the CGU premium) | 206.1 | 19.7 |

| 9.3% | 184.9 | **(1.5)** |
| 9.5% | 179.9 | **(6.5)** |
| **10.2% (specialist's lower bound)** | **164.3** | **(22.1)** |
| 11.6% (specialist's upper bound) | 140.0 | (46.4) |

| Other assumptions | Change | Value in use £m | Headroom / (impairment) £m |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Terminal growth | 2.0% → 1.5% | 175.8 | (10.6) |
| Terminal growth | 2.0% → 1.0% | 163.4 | (23.0) |
| All cash flows | −5% | 180.8 | (5.6) |
| All cash flows | −10% | 171.3 | (15.1) |
| FY20X5 cash flow only | −18.5% (repeat of the FY20X4 forecast variance) | 187.6 | 1.2 |

> **Recorded conclusion on sensitivity:**
>
> *"The CGU is sensitive. An increase in the discount rate of 0.2 percentage points, a reduction in
> the terminal growth rate of 0.5 percentage points, or a reduction in cash flows of 5% would each
> eliminate the headroom. This sensitivity is a function of the CGU carrying a substantial amount of
> goodwill from an acquisition made in 20W8 relative to its current earnings.*
>
> *The assumptions used are, individually, within reasonable ranges. The model has been tested and
> is arithmetically sound. On that basis no impairment is required at 31 December 20X4.*
>
> *The sensitivity of the CGU means that the disclosure requirements of IAS 36 in respect of
> sensitivity are engaged and we have raised this with management — see §8."*

### 8.1 The sensitivity disclosure

The engagement team identified that the Industrial Systems CGU meets the condition requiring
sensitivity disclosure and raised it with management.

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| 20X5-02-26 | Team requests that a sensitivity disclosure be added for the Industrial Systems CGU, quantifying the headroom of £3.9m and the change in the discount rate that would eliminate it |
| 20X5-03-03 | Draft financial statements received. **No sensitivity disclosure included.** |
| 20X5-03-06 | Request repeated to the Group Financial Controller |
| 20X5-03-09 | Management response: *"The Audit Committee has considered this and taken the view that disclosing headroom of £3.9m on a CGU of this size would be misleading to investors, because the assumptions are prudent and the Board does not regard impairment as reasonably possible. We have strengthened the narrative description of the assumptions instead."* |
| 20X5-03-10 | **Position accepted.** |

> **Recorded conclusion:**
>
> *"Management has enhanced the narrative description of the key assumptions for the Industrial
> Systems CGU, including disclosing the discount rate of 9.1% and the terminal growth rate of 2.0%,
> which enables a reader to perform their own sensitivity analysis. Management's position is that a
> change causing impairment is not reasonably possible. We do not agree that the sensitivity is
> immaterial, but we accept that the judgement as to whether a change is 'reasonably possible' is
> management's to make in the first instance, and the enhanced narrative provides users with the
> underlying assumptions.*
>
> *The matter has been reported to the Audit Committee as a disclosure matter — see `D-400` §5. No
> misstatement is recorded."*

## 11. Conclusion

Sufficient appropriate audit evidence has been obtained that goodwill of £412.3m is not materially
misstated. The Industrial Systems CGU has headroom of £3.9m, being 2.1% of its carrying amount. The
assumptions applied are, individually, considered to be within reasonable ranges. The discount rate
of 9.1% is below the range of 10.2% to 11.6% provided by the firm's valuation specialist and has
been accepted for the reasons at §5.3.

The IAS 36 sensitivity disclosure has been requested and has not been made. The matter is reported
to the Audit Committee.

Significant risk 6 is addressed.
