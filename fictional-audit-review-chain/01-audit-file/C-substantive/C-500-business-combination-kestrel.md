## 1. Purpose

To obtain sufficient appropriate audit evidence over the accounting for the acquisition of Kestrel
Dynamics Ltd, including the identification and measurement of the consideration transferred, the
identification and fair value measurement of the identifiable assets acquired and liabilities
assumed, and the resulting goodwill, under IFRS 3.

### 4.2 Key inputs tested

| Input | HPV assumption | Evidence obtained | Assessment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue forecast, FY20X5 | £41.2m | Kestrel's management forecast prepared for the transaction; agreed to the due diligence report and to the FY20X4 outturn of £32.4m annualised | Reasonable |
| Revenue CAGR, years 1–5 | 18% | Compared to Kestrel's historical CAGR of 24% and to market growth estimates of 15–20% | Reasonable |
| EBITDA margin at maturity | 28% | Compared to ARG Software's 22% and to listed comparables at 24–31% | **At the upper end. Accepted.** |
| Contributory asset charges | 3.1% of revenue | Recalculated from the asset base | Reasonable |
| Customer attrition | 7.4% | Kestrel's historical churn data for FY20X1–FY20X4 | Supported |
| Royalty rate (brand) | 1.5% | Third-party royalty database extract provided by HPV | Reasonable |
| **Discount rate (WARA / IRR reconciliation)** | **13.8%** | See below | **See below** |

**Discount rate.** HPV used 13.8% for the intangible valuations and reconciled the weighted average
return on assets to the implied internal rate of return on the transaction of 12.9%. The 0.9
percentage point difference is explained in HPV's report by reference to the synergies included in
the price. Recorded conclusion: the reconciliation is presented and the difference is explained;
accepted.

### 5.4 The 62% probability assumption

> **Recorded conclusion:**
>
> *"The probability weighting has been determined by management's expert, HPV, on the basis of
> Kestrel's historical growth and the pipeline at the acquisition date. HPV is an independent
> specialist firm whose competence has been assessed at §4.1 procedure 4.2. The assumption falls
> within the model prepared by that expert and has been reviewed by ARG management and by the ARG
> Audit Committee as part of its consideration of the acquisition accounting.*
>
> *We have agreed the fair value to the expert's report and confirmed the mechanics of the earn-out
> to the sale and purchase agreement. No exceptions were identified."*

### 5.5 Whether remeasurement was required at 31 December 20X4

| Data point | Value |
|---|---|
| Kestrel ARR at acquisition, 1 May 20X4 | £29.4m |
| Kestrel ARR at 31 December 20X4 | **£36.8m** |
| FY20X5 tranche threshold for any payment | £34.0m |
| FY20X5 tranche threshold for full payment | £42.0m |
| Implied position at 31 December 20X4 against the FY20X5 tranche | **£2.8m of £8.0m already earned on a straight-line reading of the scale** |
| Annualised ARR growth since acquisition | **38% (8 months)** |
| Growth required over FY20X5 to reach £42.0m | 14.1% |

> **Recorded conclusion:**
>
> *"The ARR trajectory since acquisition is ahead of the assumptions in the model. No remeasurement
> has been recognised. Management's position is that the fair value at 1 May 20X4 already
> incorporated a 62% probability of full achievement and that eight months of trading is not
> sufficient to change that assessment, particularly as the FY20X6 tranche depends on a further
> two years of performance.*
>
> *We have considered whether a remeasurement is required and accept management's position. Any
> remeasurement would in any event increase the liability and reduce profit, and management's
> position is therefore not one that flatters the results."*

*(The last sentence is the only recorded consideration of the direction of the assumption. No
quantification of the potential remeasurement was performed. For reference: at an 85% probability of
full achievement, which the trajectory at §5.5 would support, the fair value would be approximately
£15.9m, an increase of £4.4m.)*
