## 3. Previously unidentified related party — Larkspur Freight Ltd

### 3.1 How the matter arose

During journal entry and vendor testing at `C-700` §6.1, the preparer performed a companies registry
search on new vendors receiving payments above £250k. One such vendor was **Larkspur Freight Ltd**.

| Registry search result | Detail |
|---|---|
| Company | Larkspur Freight Ltd, company number 09241188 |
| Incorporated | 20X1 |
| **Sole director** | **Gerald Thwaite-Marchant** |
| **Sole shareholder** | **Gerald Thwaite-Marchant (100 ordinary shares)** |
| Registered office | 4 Rowan Court, Ashley Vale, Meridia AV1 3RQ |
| Latest filed accounts (FY20X3) | Turnover £3.1m, 11 employees |
| Filed accounts FY20X4 (filed 20X5-06) | *Not available at the audit report date* |

### 3.2 The connection

The registered office address of Larkspur Freight Ltd, **4 Rowan Court, Ashley Vale**, matches the
address recorded for **Mrs Helena Thwaite-Marchant** in the related party questionnaire completed by
**Ms Imogen Castellanos-Reyes, Chief Financial Officer**.

In that questionnaire (filed at `C-800/3`), Ms Castellanos-Reyes lists under "close family members":

| Name | Relationship | Address |
|---|---|---|
| (spouse's name redacted in the questionnaire copy on file) | Spouse | — |
| **Mrs Helena Thwaite-Marchant** | **Sister** | **4 Rowan Court, Ashley Vale, Meridia AV1 3RQ** |
| (two further names) | Children | — |

The questionnaire asks, at question 6: *"Are you aware of any transaction between the Group and any
entity in which you or a close family member has a material interest?"* Ms Castellanos-Reyes answered
**"No"**.

**Conclusion drawn by the preparer:** Gerald Thwaite-Marchant is the husband of the CFO's sister, and
therefore the CFO's brother-in-law. Larkspur Freight Ltd is an entity controlled by him.

### 3.3 The transactions

| | £m |
|---|---:|
| **Payments to Larkspur Freight Ltd in FY20X4** | **2.40** |
| Number of invoices | 14 |
| Balance outstanding at 31 December 20X4 | 0.18 |
| First payment | 14 March 20X4 |
| Payments in FY20X3 | **nil** |
| **Specific materiality for related party transactions** | **0.100** |
| **Multiple of specific materiality** | **24×** |

From `C-700` §6.1 and §6.2:

- Larkspur was not on the approved supplier list at 1 January 20X4; it was created on 6 March 20X4
- A single-source justification form was completed, signed by the Head of Logistics. No tender.
- 3 of 14 invoices had no purchase order
- Rates appear to be **4–7% above** two comparable hauliers already on the approved list
- **Three payments totalling £680k were made outside the standard payment run**, in the two weeks
  either side of the ERP go-live, without documented exception approval

### 3.5 Discussion with the CFO

By telephone, 24 February 20X5. File note at `C-800/4`.

> *"Emeka raised Larkspur. IC-R's response, as I recorded it at the time:*
>
> *'Larkspur is a haulage business that has worked for our Kirkwall depot for years as a
> subcontractor to our main carrier. When we restructured the logistics contracts in early 20X4 the
> Head of Logistics brought them on directly because they know the routes and the depot. Yes, Gerald
> is married to my sister. I had nothing to do with the appointment — you should speak to
> Logistics — and the rates are commercial. In any case he is not a close family member of mine, he
> is an in-law, so I did not consider the questionnaire question to be engaged. The relationship is
> by marriage and I do not have and never have had any interest in his company.'*
>
> *I asked whether she had disclosed the relationship to the Audit Committee. She said she had not,
> because there was nothing to disclose."*

### 3.6 Discussion with the engagement manager and conclusion recorded

The preparer raised the matter with the engagement manager on 26 February 20X5.

> **Conclusion as recorded on the workpaper:**
>
> *"Discussed with CFO who confirmed Larkspur is a long-standing haulage supplier engaged on arm's
> length terms following competitive tender. Relationship is by marriage and not a close family
> member per the CFO. No disclosure required. Cleared."*

*(Preparer's note added below the conclusion: "SL has concluded on this. For the record, two points I
raised: (1) the file does not contain a competitive tender — there is a single-source justification
form; (2) my rate comparison shows Larkspur 4–7% higher than the two comparators. SL's view is that
a 4–7% rate difference on £2.4m is not evidence of a non-commercial arrangement and that the
disclosure question turns on whether the CFO is a related party of Larkspur, which she is not, as she
has no interest in it. EB 26/02/X5.")*
