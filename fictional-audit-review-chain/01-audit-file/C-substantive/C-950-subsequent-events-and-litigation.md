## 5. The Meridian Rail Authority delay claim (L1)

### 5.1 The facts

| | |
|---|---|
| **Date notice served** | **4 February 20X5** |
| Claimant | Meridian Rail Authority |
| Contract | **CN-4471, Northline Rolling Stock Retrofit** |
| Basis of claim | Liquidated damages under clause 34.2 for failure to achieve delivery gates 3 and 4 by the contractual dates |
| Contractual LD rate | £0.085m per week per gate, capped at 10% of the contract price |
| Weeks claimed | Gate 3: 41 weeks. Gate 4: 39 weeks. |
| **Amount claimed** | **£6.80m** |
| Cap under the contract | £9.64m (10% of £96.4m) |
| ARG's response | Notice of dispute served 18 February 20X5, asserting that the delays are attributable to MRA-caused changes in the certification protocol and to late release of vehicles for retrofit, both of which entitle ARG to extensions of time |

### 5.2 Evidence obtained

| Evidence | Obtained |
|---|---|
| The MRA notice of claim, 4 February 20X5 | ✔ `C-950/4` |
| ARG's notice of dispute, 18 February 20X5 | ✔ `C-950/5` |
| Legal adviser's response (Hartsmere Dowd LLP) | ✔ `C-950/3` |
| Programme correspondence on extensions of time | ✔ 14 items read |
| Management's assessment | ✔ Memorandum from the General Counsel, 2 March 20X5 |
| Contract clause 34.2 and the extension of time provisions | ✔ Read |

**The legal adviser's letter states:**

> *"The claim was served on 4 February 20X5 and our client served a notice of dispute on 18 February
> 20X5. The claim is at an early stage. Our client's position that the delays are substantially
> attributable to the Authority's own actions is, on the material we have reviewed, arguable, and we
> note that the Authority's variation of the certification protocol in Q3 20X4 is documented. However,
> the extension of time claims have not been formally submitted or agreed, and the strength of the
> position will depend on the contemporaneous programme records.*
>
> *We are not able at this stage to express a view on the likely outcome or quantum with any
> confidence. We would not describe an adverse outcome as remote."*

### 5.3 Management's assessment and the accounting conclusion

Management's position, per the General Counsel's memorandum:

> *"The claim is without merit. The delays arise from the Authority's own change to the certification
> protocol and its late release of vehicles. We will resist the claim in full and expect to succeed. No
> provision is appropriate. We accept that disclosure as a contingent liability is required."*

**Audit assessment recorded:**

| Question | Conclusion |
|---|---|
| Is a present obligation probable? | **Not probable.** ARG has an arguable defence on causation, documented in the programme correspondence. The legal adviser does not describe an adverse outcome as probable. |
| Can the amount be reliably estimated? | Not at this stage |
| **Is a provision required under IAS 37?** | **No** |
| Is a contingent liability disclosure required? | **Yes** — the legal adviser does not describe an adverse outcome as remote |
| Is the disclosure made? | ✔ Note 25 discloses the claim, the amount claimed and management's assessment |
| Is the claim an adjusting or non-adjusting event? | The claim was served after the year end but relates to delays that had occurred before it. **Assessed as providing evidence of a condition existing at the balance sheet date and therefore relevant to measurement at the year end.** See §5.4. |

### 5.4 Effect on measurement at the balance sheet date

| Question | Conclusion recorded |
|---|---|
| Does the claim indicate that the contract CN-4471 transaction price should be constrained? | *"The claim is a counterclaim against ARG, not a reduction in the consideration to which ARG is entitled. Variable consideration under IFRS 15 has been considered at `C-100` §8.1 and no adjustment is required."* |
| Does the claim indicate that the contract is loss-making? | *"No. The forecast margin on CN-4471 is 22.4%, being £21.6m on a contract price of £96.4m. Even if the claim succeeded in full at £6.80m the contract would remain profitable."* |
| Does the claim indicate that the contract asset of £34.7m is impaired? | *"No. Amounts billed and billable are supported by the delivery gates achieved. The claim does not affect ARG's entitlement to payment for work performed."* |
| **Does the claim indicate that the estimate of costs to complete may be understated?** | **Not addressed** |
| Does the claim affect the going concern assessment? | *"No. The amount is not material to liquidity."* |

*(On the fourth row: the claim asserts that ARG is 41 and 39 weeks late on two delivery gates. In the
same quarter in which those delays crystallised, management reduced its estimate of the cost of
completing the contract by £4.1m, on the stated grounds of value engineering and a reduction in
certification scope — the latter arising from the same MRA protocol change that ARG relies upon as
its defence to the delay claim. The relationship between a 40-week delay and a £4.1m cost reduction
is not considered in this workpaper, in `C-100`, or anywhere else in the file. `C-100` §8.1
cross-refers to this workpaper; this workpaper cross-refers to `C-100` §6 only for the margin figure
used at §5.4 above.)*
