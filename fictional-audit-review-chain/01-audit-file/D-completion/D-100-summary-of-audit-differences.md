### 3.2 Qualitative evaluation

ISA 450 requires the evaluation of whether uncorrected misstatements are material to consider size
**and** nature, and the particular circumstances of their occurrence.

| Qualitative factor | Assessment recorded |
|---|---|
| Do the misstatements affect compliance with a debt covenant? | **No.** The covenant is measured on Adjusted EBITDA. U1 would reduce Adjusted EBITDA by £1.900m, moving leverage from 2.47× to 2.50×. Covenant limit 3.00×. No breach. |
| Do they affect compliance with a regulatory requirement? | No |
| **Do they affect a measure on which management remuneration is based?** | **U1, U2 and U3 all affect adjusted EPS.** Combined effect 4.1p, moving adjusted EPS from 149.2p to 145.1p. The CFO's LTIP threshold is 138.0p and maximum 156.0p. **The vesting outcome moves from 62% to 39% of maximum.** |
| Do they mask a change in earnings or other trends? | Correcting all three would reduce PBT growth from 8.1% to 4.1%. Considered; the growth trend remains positive. |
| Do they affect segment information? | U1 falls in Aftermarket Services. Segment margin would move from 8.3% to 7.8%. |
| Do they relate to the concealment of an unlawful transaction? | No |
| Do they arise from an item capable of precise measurement (in which case the tolerance is lower)? | **Yes — all three are factual, precisely measured amounts.** |
| Is the effect of an offsetting nature? | U1 reverses in FY20X5; U2 and U3 also reverse |
| **Is there a risk of further undetected misstatement of the same type?** | **See §3.3** |

### 3.3 Risk of further undetected misstatement of the same type

> **Recorded assessment:**
>
> *"U1 arises from three errors identified in a sample of 40 items drawn from the cut-off population.
> The errors arise from a site-level invoicing practice. We have considered whether other items in the
> population are similarly affected.*
>
> *The three errors are the actual errors we identified and have been included at their precise value.
> The remaining 37 items in the sample were tested and found to be correct. We have no basis to assert
> that any specific further item is misstated.*
>
> *We have also performed the additional cut-off procedures at `C-200` §7 — a credit note review, a
> daily revenue analytical review, and a review of manual revenue journals in the cut-off window — none
> of which identified further misstatement.*
>
> *We are satisfied that the aggregate of £2.370m represents the misstatement in the financial
> statements."*

### 3.4 The projection question

Firm methodology, at `AV-AUD-22` §4.6, states: *"Where a misstatement is identified in a sample, the
auditor shall project the misstatement to the population from which the sample was drawn, unless the
misstatement is demonstrably anomalous. The projected misstatement, not the misstatement found, is the
amount to be included in the evaluation of misstatements."*

`C-200` §6 concludes at the second consideration that the three errors are **"not anomalous"**.

**No projection appears on this schedule.** The amount included is the factual £1.900m.

*(For reference, and not on the file as archived: ratio projection of the sample error gives
£1.900m ÷ £84.6m × £312.4m = **£7.02m**. Substituting that for U1 gives total uncorrected
misstatements of **£7.470m**, being **11.97% of profit before tax** and **2.4× Group materiality**.
The calculation was first performed by the internal inspection team — see `I-300` finding
`INSP-F02`.)*

## 6. Effect on the audit strategy

ISA 450 requires the auditor to consider whether the aggregate of misstatements identified indicates
that the audit strategy and plan need revision, and whether misstatements identified may be indicative
of fraud.

| Question | Conclusion recorded |
|---|---|
| Does the number or nature of misstatements identified indicate that further misstatement may exist? | *"Five adjustments were proposed and accepted and three remain uncorrected. This is broadly consistent with prior years (FY20X3: four corrected, two uncorrected). No revision to the strategy is required."* |
| Do any misstatements indicate fraud? | *"No. The cut-off errors at U1 have been assessed at `C-200` §5.2 as arising from an operational practice at site level rather than from a directed manipulation."* |
| Has performance materiality proved to be set at an appropriate level? | **Not addressed** |

*(On the last row: uncorrected misstatements of £2.370m exceed performance materiality of £2.170m.
Performance materiality is the amount set to reduce to an appropriately low level the probability that
the aggregate of uncorrected and undetected misstatements exceeds materiality. The aggregate of
uncorrected misstatements alone having exceeded it, the margin left for undetected misstatement is
negative. This is not considered on the schedule.)*
