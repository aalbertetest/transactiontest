## 1. Purpose and conclusion

This memorandum summarises the conduct and outcome of the audit, records the resolution of significant
matters, and supports the engagement partner's conclusion that sufficient appropriate audit evidence
has been obtained and that an unmodified opinion is appropriate.

> **Overall conclusion.** Sufficient appropriate audit evidence has been obtained to form an opinion on
> the financial statements of Auburn Ridge Group plc for the year ended 31 December 20X4. In our
> opinion the financial statements give a true and fair view and are properly prepared in accordance
> with IFRS as adopted in Meridia and the Meridian Companies Act 20W1. **An unmodified opinion is
> appropriate.** No material uncertainty related to going concern exists. Three key audit matters are
> reported.

## 3. Significant risks — how each was addressed and concluded

| # | Significant risk | Workpaper | Conclusion |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Contract revenue — estimated costs to complete** | `C-100` | Twelve contracts tested, 66.8% of segment revenue. The estimate of costs to complete on CN-4471 was reduced by £4.1m in Q4, increasing revenue and PBT by £3.5m. Management's explanation attributes the reduction to identifiable value engineering and certification scope changes, is consistent with the Group's stated strategy, and was recorded by the Contract Review Board in December 20X4. **Concluded: within a reasonable range.** |
| 2 | **Revenue cut-off (presumed fraud risk)** | `C-200` | 40 items, £84.6m, from a population of 2,317 items, £312.4m. Three errors totalling £1.90m identified, arising from a site-level invoicing practice, assessed as error not fraud, reported and included in full on `D-100`. **Concluded: appropriately addressed.** |
| 3 | **Software revenue — multi-element and agency** | `C-300` | 30 arrangements tested. Reseller channel of £38.2m recognised gross; management's principal conclusion accepted. **Concluded: appropriate.** |
| 4 | **Management override of controls** | `C-700` | 60 of 214 CAAT-selected items tested from a manual journal population of 42,118. Seven control exceptions and one matter requiring investigation, all explained. **Concluded: no indication of override.** |
| 5 | **Business combination** | `C-500` | Consideration of £86.0m, net identifiable assets of £34.8m and goodwill of £51.2m. Firm valuation specialist engaged on the intangibles. **Concluded: appropriate.** |
| 6 | **Goodwill impairment** | `C-600` | Industrial Systems CGU headroom £3.9m (2.1%). Discount rate of 9.1% accepted, below the specialist's range of 10.2%–11.6%. Sensitivity disclosure requested and not made; reported to the Audit Committee. **Concluded: no impairment required.** |
| 7 | **Going concern** | `C-900` | Base case compliant at all four quarterly test dates; downside case as summarised also compliant. RCF matures 27 Feb 20X6, outside the period assessed. **Concluded: no material uncertainty.** |
| 8 | **Related party completeness** | `C-800` | One previously unidentified vendor relationship identified and discussed with the CFO. **Concluded: no disclosable related party transaction.** |
| 9 | **IT general controls** | `B-200` | Eight deficiencies in the Aventine environment, four significant. **Concluded: controls reliance remains appropriate for the full year.** |
