## 3. Scope of the review

Under ISQM 2 and firm methodology `AV-EQR-01`, the engagement quality reviewer shall:

1. Discuss significant matters and significant judgements with the engagement partner
2. Review selected engagement documentation relating to the significant judgements made and the
   conclusions reached
3. Review the financial statements and the proposed auditor's report
4. Evaluate the basis for the engagement partner's determination that independence requirements have
   been fulfilled
5. Evaluate whether appropriate consultation has taken place on difficult or contentious matters and
   the conclusions arising
6. **Notify the engagement partner if the review cannot be completed, in which case the report shall
   not be dated**

### 5.2 Contract revenue — reviewer's comment (recorded 14 March 20X5)

> *"I have reviewed `C-100`. The change in estimate on CN-4471 increases revenue and profit by £3.5m,
> which is above materiality. The evidence supporting the change is management's own schedule, an
> email from the project director and a note of a telephone call. There is no third-party or
> engineering corroboration and no look-back on the accuracy of prior estimates.*
>
> *I raised this with RB on 11 March. His position is that the explanation is specific and
> attributable, that the Contract Review Board minuted the change, and that the team has read the
> contract and tested the costs incurred. He also notes that the delay claim served in February has
> been considered separately at `C-950` and does not make the contract loss-making.*
>
> *I would have wanted to see the look-back analysis. Having discussed it, I accept that this is a
> matter of degree rather than a conclusion that is unsupportable, and I do not consider it a basis
> for me to withhold my concurrence. **I have recorded it as a matter for the FY20X5 audit.**"*
> **`EQR-03`**

### 5.3 Goodwill — reviewer's comment (recorded 14 March 20X5)

> *"Headroom of £3.9m on a carrying amount of £186.4m, and management's discount rate is below our own
> specialist's range. The team's conclusion at `C-600` §5.3 is that 9.1% is 'at the low end but within
> a reasonable range'. That is not accurate: it is below the range. The workpaper should say so.*
>
> *Discussed with RB on 11 March. He notes that the specialist's range is derived from comparable
> companies rather than ARG's actual cost of capital, and that the disclosure now includes the
> discount rate so a reader can form their own view.*
>
> *I am uncomfortable with the sensitivity disclosure not being made and I am pleased to see it
> reported to the Audit Committee. On the discount rate, I accept RB's judgement. **Matter for
> FY20X5.**"* **`EQR-04`**

### 5.4 Going concern — reviewer's comment (recorded 14 March 20X5)

> *"The period of assessment point is a real one and I see it was raised by the manager at `MRN-031`
> and cleared on the basis that management would extend the wording. Extending the wording is not the
> same as extending the assessment. There is no forecast on the file covering to 31 March 20X6.*
>
> *RB tells me he discussed this with the National Technical Panel on 10 March and that their view was
> that, where the additional period contains no covenant test that the base case trajectory would fail
> and no maturity within the assessment horizon that the entity cannot meet, the substance is
> adequately addressed. I have not seen a written record of that consultation.*
>
> *I note the RCF matures 27 February 20X6, which is inside the extended period. RB's view is that the
> refinancing is a matter of confidence rather than doubt given the rating and the compliance record.*
>
> *On balance I do not dissent. **I have recorded this as the single most significant judgement in the
> file and as a priority matter for FY20X5.**"* **`EQR-05`**
