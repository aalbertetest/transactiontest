## 1. Purpose

To record the assembly of the final audit file within the period required by ISA 230 and firm policy
`AV-AUD-19`, and to log any modification to the file after assembly.

## 2. Policy requirements

`AV-AUD-19`:

> *"Audit files shall be assembled within **60 days** of the report date. On completion of assembly the
> engagement partner shall lock the file in the engagement management system. Post-assembly modification
> requires documented approval by the engagement partner **and** the Head of Quality, recording the
> reason for the change, its nature, who made it and when. No workpaper shall be deleted after
> assembly."*

ISA 230 provides to similar effect, with 60 days being the period the firm has adopted as an appropriate
time limit.

### 4.2 Modification 8 — deficiency severity downgrade

Deficiency D-12 ("vendor master data changes not independently verified") was recorded at `B-300` §6 as
**Significant**. It was one of the five deficiencies described as significant in that log, though it was
not among the four reported to the Audit Committee at `D-400` §8.

`B-300` §8 contained a note explaining that D-12 did not appear in `D-400`. That note was not removed and
remains on the file, now inconsistent with the log it refers to.
