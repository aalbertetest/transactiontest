## 3. Identification of components

Under ISA 600 (Revised) the group engagement team determines the nature, timing and extent of work
on components by reference to the risks of material misstatement in the group financial statements,
not solely by reference to size.

### 3.1 Risk-based assessment

| Entity | Financial significance | Specific risk of material misstatement | Conclusion |
|---|---|---|---|
| ARG Systems Ltd | 44% of revenue | **CN-4471 and the rail portfolio; significant risk 1** | **Full scope** |
| ARG Polska | 19% of revenue | Manufacturing cost absorption feeding contract costs | **Full scope** |
| ARG Software + Kestrel | 8% of revenue | **Significant risks 3 and 5 (agency; PPA)** | **Full scope** |
| ARG Systems GmbH | 12% of revenue | Contract portfolio, but smaller and less complex; no single contract above £14m | **Specified procedures** |
| ARG Aftermarket Inc | 15% of revenue | High volume, low judgement; **significant risk 2 (cut-off) partly resides here** | **Analytical procedures at group level, plus group-team cut-off testing** |
| Parent | Treasury, central | Going concern, borrowings, share-based payment | **Audited by the group team** |
| Dormant / immaterial | <0.3% | None | Analytical procedures |

### 3.2 Coverage achieved

| Scope | Revenue | PBT | Total assets |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Full scope | **71%** | **68%** | 74% |
| Specified procedures | 12% | 14% | 8% |
| Analytical procedures only | 17% | 18% | 18% |

**Consideration of the coverage.** Full-scope coverage of 71% of revenue is below the 75% the firm's
methodology describes as typical for a listed group. The gap is driven by the decision to apply
analytical procedures only to ARG Aftermarket Inc (15% of revenue).

Rationale for that decision:

1. Aftermarket Americas has no significant risk of material misstatement specific to it other than
   cut-off. Its revenue is high-volume, low-value, point-in-time.
2. **The cut-off risk is being addressed by the group engagement team directly**, through `C-200`,
   using a group-wide population. The group team's cut-off sample is drawn from all Aftermarket
   locations including Delrado, so the risk is covered without a component audit.
3. The entity's contribution to PBT (£10.0m) is 16% of group PBT, and a component materiality
   below group materiality would produce a component audit of limited incremental value against a
   cost of approximately £110k.
4. Prior year experience: no misstatements identified in Delrado in FY20X1–FY20X3.

**The group engagement partner has approved this scoping.** It is recorded here that the decision
depends on the group team's cut-off testing at `C-200` genuinely covering the Delrado population.

*(Updated 20X5-03-04: `C-200` confirms the sample was drawn from the group despatch population
including Delrado. Of the 40 items sampled, 6 were Delrado despatches, of which 1 was an error.)*
