### 4.3 The operational efficiency review

**Scope as engaged.** A review of the Group's order-to-cash and procure-to-pay processes, with
recommendations for process simplification, conducted by the firm's consulting practice
(engagement partner: L. Farrand-Whyte) between July and November 20X4.

**Deliverables.** A 68-page report dated 21 November 20X4 and a management presentation.

**Content relevant to the audit.** Sections 4 and 6 of the consulting report contain
recommendations on **the configuration of approval workflows and authorisation limits within
Aventine ERP**, including recommended changes to the segregation of duties matrix for the
purchase-to-pay cycle. ARG implemented eleven of the fourteen recommendations between September
and December 20X4.

**Threats identified:**

| Threat | Analysis |
|---|---|
| **Self-review** | The audit team will test and may seek to rely upon internal controls whose design was influenced by recommendations from the firm's consulting practice. This is a self-review threat to the audit of the control environment and of any balance where controls reliance is placed. |
| **Management responsibility** | Whether the firm assumed a management responsibility by designing controls. Assessed as **not assumed**: the consulting engagement letter states that all decisions on implementation rest with management, and ARG's own IT and finance functions performed the configuration. |
| Advocacy | Not applicable. |
| Self-interest | The £0.32m fee is 0.4% of the Verrastown office's annual fee income. Not significant. |

**Safeguards applied:**

1. Entirely separate personnel. No member of the consulting team is or has been a member of the
   audit engagement team, and vice versa.
2. Pre-approval by the Audit Committee on 12 June 20X4, following disclosure of the intended scope.
3. The audit engagement partner has reviewed the consulting report and confirmed that it contains
   no conclusion on the operating effectiveness of any control.
4. **The audit approach will not place reliance on any control whose design was materially altered
   as a result of the consulting recommendations.** The IT audit lead (Y. Demirci) has been
   provided with the consulting report and instructed to identify affected controls.

### 4.4 Ethics Partner consultation

Consulted: **D. Marchetti-Boyle**, Ethics Partner, on 26 September 20X4. Consultation memorandum
filed at `A-200/4`.

Matters referred:

1. The breach of firm policy `AV-IND-02` (43.0% against a 40% cap).
2. The self-review threat arising from the operational efficiency review.

**Ethics Partner conclusion, as recorded in the memorandum:**

> *"The statutory cap is not breached and the three-year average is comfortably within tolerance.
> The firm policy limit is exceeded by 3 percentage points in a single year, driven by a one-off
> advisory engagement that will not recur; the ratio will normalise in FY20X5. The self-review
> threat is real but is capable of being reduced to an acceptable level by the safeguards proposed,
> in particular the commitment not to rely on affected controls. I am content that objectivity and
> independence are not compromised. The breach of AV-IND-02 should be reported in the firm's annual
> independence compliance return.*
>
> *I would note for the file that the safeguard at 4.3(4) is only meaningful if it is actually
> operated. The engagement partner should satisfy himself at completion that the IT audit workpaper
> identifies the affected controls and that no reliance has been placed on them."*

**Action arising:** Engagement partner to confirm at completion that safeguard 4.3(4) has operated.

## 7. Conclusion

The firm and the engagement team are independent of Auburn Ridge Group plc for the year ended
31 December 20X4 in accordance with the IESBA Code and the Meridian ethical standard.

One breach of internal firm policy `AV-IND-02` is recorded and has been reported through the firm's
independence compliance process. The breach does not, in the judgement of the Ethics Partner and
the engagement partner, compromise independence in fact or in appearance.

The matter has been communicated to the Audit Committee — see `D-400` §7.
