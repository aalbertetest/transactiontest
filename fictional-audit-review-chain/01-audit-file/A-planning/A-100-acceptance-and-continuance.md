### 4.2 Business and financial reporting risk

| Factor | FY20X3 | FY20X4 | Commentary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Complexity of accounting | High | **High** | Long-term contracts; multi-element software arrangements |
| Estimation uncertainty | High | **High** | Contract cost estimates; goodwill; now contingent consideration |
| Financial leverage | Moderate | **Elevated** | Net debt/EBITDA rising from 2.21× to a forecast 2.5×; acquisition-funded |
| Growth and market pressure | Moderate | **Elevated** | Software segment growth is a stated strategic priority and features in investor communications |
| Management incentive arrangements | Moderate | **Elevated** | CFO LTIP measured on adjusted EPS over FY20X3–X5; final measurement year is FY20X5 |
| Systems stability | Stable | **Elevated** | Aventine ERP go-live 1 July 20X4 |
| Corporate activity | None | **Yes** | Kestrel Dynamics acquired 1 May 20X4 |

**Four risk factors moved adversely this year.** The engagement partner has considered whether the
overall client risk classification should move from Elevated to High. The conclusion is that
Elevated remains appropriate because the underlying business is cash-generative, the governance
structure is strong, and no individual factor is at an extreme.

*(Manager's note, added at planning: I would have been comfortable with High. RB's view is that we
would then be required to schedule a second partner review at interim, which the fee does not
support and which he does not consider proportionate. Recorded here for completeness. SL 11/09/X4.)*

**Resourcing.** The FY20X4 reporting timetable is unchanged from prior year (report date 12 March
20X5). The engagement senior in charge from FY20X2 and FY20X3, M. Farrow, has transferred to the
firm's Delrado office. D. Ramanathan has been assigned in his place. Mr Ramanathan has four years'
experience and has previously worked on two listed audits as a senior team member, though not as
senior in charge.

The engagement partner has considered whether this represents a resourcing risk and concluded that
it does not, on the basis that the manager (S. Lindqvist) has two years of continuity on the
engagement and will provide additional direction and supervision. An additional 40 hours has been
added to the manager's budget for this purpose.

*(The manager's budget for FY20X4 is 158 hours, against 118 recorded in FY20X3. Actual hours
recorded in FY20X4 were 118.0 — see `A-700` §6 and `D-200` §9.)*
