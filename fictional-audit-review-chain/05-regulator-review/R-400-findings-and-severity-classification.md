## 2. The distinction the FRAOA maintains

**Every finding below is a finding that evidence was not obtained. None is a finding that the financial
statements were misstated.**

The FRAOA has not audited ARG and does not opine on its financial statements. Where an amount is quantified,
it is the amount in respect of which evidence was lacking, measured at the adverse end of a defensible range.
The aggregate of £35.1m at `R-300` §5.1 states the exposure the audit left unresolved, not an assertion that
profit was overstated by that sum.

## `FRAOA-F01` — Auditor's report dated before completion of the engagement quality review · **S2**

**Standards:** ISQM 2; ISA 220 (Revised); `AV-EQR-01` §9 · **Issue:** `ISS-09` · **Against:** engagement and firm

EQR sign-offs on `C-100`, `C-600` and `C-900` — three workpapers addressing significant risks — are timestamped
**20X5-03-14**, two days after the report was dated on 20X5-03-12. Five hours of EQR time were recorded on
14 March.

The FRAOA accepts that the substantive discussions took place on 11 March and that both individuals gave
consistent accounts. **The requirement is not that the reviewer form a view before the report is dated; it is
that the review be complete.** A review of a significant-risk workpaper is not complete before the workpaper has
been read.

**Systemic:** present in **3 of the 7 engagements** the firm inspected in cycle 20X5. The system does not
prevent it and produces no report showing an engagement partner which sign-offs are outstanding. The remedial
action has been agreed in **three successive cycles and deferred twice**. See `FRAOA-F15`.

## `FRAOA-F05` — Contingent consideration: no audit procedure performed on an £11.5m Level 3 fair value · **S1**

**Standards:** ISA 540 (Revised); ISA 620; IFRS 13 · **Issue:** `ISS-05`

| | |
|---|---:|
| Contingent consideration at fair value | **£11.5m** |

| Fair value hierarchy | **Level 3** |
| Key unobservable input | 62% probability of full earn-out |

**Procedures performed:** the amount was agreed to management's expert's report; the mechanics were agreed to

**Procedures performed on the measurement itself: none.**

**The scope gap.** The firm's valuation specialist's scope letter (`C-500/4`) covers *"the identification and

**`C-500` was reviewed by nobody.** The manager did not open it, believing the specialist had covered the
acquisition. The partner did not open it, believing the same. **Three people each drew a reasonable inference
and an £11.5m Level 3 fair value was audited by none of them.**

**Contrary evidence on the file:** ARR rose from £29.4m to £36.8m in eight months against thresholds of £34.0m
and £42.0m — 38% annualised against 14.1% required. `C-500` §5.5 sets out that data and concludes no
remeasurement is required.

`AV-TAP-04`(d) required mandatory consultation on a Level 3 fair value exceeding Group materiality. Not
performed. See `FRAOA-F11`.

## `FRAOA-F07` — Going concern: downside case covenant breach not tested · **S1**

**Standards:** ISA 570 (Revised); ISA 500 · **Issue:** `ISS-07`

| Test date | Downside leverage | Covenant | Visible in the version filed |
|---|---:|---:|:-:|
| 31 Mar 20X5 | 2.79× | ≤3.00× | ✔ |
| 30 Jun 20X5 | 2.96× | ≤3.00× | ✔ |
| **30 Sep 20X5** | **3.08× — BREACH** | ≤3.00× | **✖ column AK hidden** |
| 31 Dec 20X5 | 2.91× | ≤3.00× | **✖ column AJ hidden** |

The two test dates on the summary tab the team reviewed are the two least stressed of the four.

The base case was recalculated at all four test dates. **The downside case was recalculated at none.**
`MRN-033` asked for exactly that calculation and was closed with the same summary tab, self-cleared by the
preparer.

**Note 2 of the financial statements states that *"in the downside scenario the Group continues to operate
within its covenants throughout the assessment period"*. That assertion is false on the face of management's
own model, and the auditor reported on it without testing it.**

**The FRAOA makes no finding of concealment by management.** Hidden columns are common in working models and
management's Board paper presented the same two dates. The finding is that a quarterly covenant was audited by
recalculating one scenario at four dates and the other at none.

**Against the firm.** Downgraded from a finding on the basis that *"the scenario did not eventuate"*.
**The purpose of a downside case is to test what happens if the downside occurs. That it did not occur is not a
reason for not having audited it.** On that reasoning no downside case would ever require audit.

## `FRAOA-F13` — Unauthorised modification of the archived audit file, and reliance on a post-assembly document to downgrade a finding · **S2**

**The critical modification.** `C-100/13`, a two-page look-back analysis, was added on **20X5-05-19** — 68 days
