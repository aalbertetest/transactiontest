# Chapter Outlines and Scope Boundaries

This file allocates subject matter across the twenty chapters. Its purpose is to prevent overlap: each
chapter owns its topics and defers the rest by cross-reference. Where two chapters touch the same subject,
the "owns" column decides who develops it and who points to it.

A chapter author should read: (1) `docs/style-guide.md`, (2) `case-materials/atlasflow-continuing-case.md`,
(3) this file's entry for their chapter, and (4) this file's entries for the two chapters on either side.

---

## Chapter 1 — Audit Planning for SaaS Engagements

**Owns:** engagement acceptance and continuance; independence in a SaaS context; engagement letters and the
integrated-audit scope; the audit strategy versus the audit plan; understanding a SaaS business model well
enough to audit it (ARR, ACV, TCV, NRR, GRR, RPO, bookings-billings-revenue-cash bridge, CAC, magic number,
Rule of 40, cohort and churn analysis); the unit economics and how they create accounting pressure;
scoping the integrated audit (Chapters 11–14 own the ICFR mechanics); group audit scoping and component
materiality mechanics (Chapter 3 owns the materiality numbers); timing, interim versus year-end, roll-forward
strategy; using specialists and the work of internal audit; supervision and review; documentation of the plan;
first-year-as-large-accelerated-filer planning implications; the audit committee planning communication.

**Defers to:** Chapter 2 (risk identification and assessment mechanics), Chapter 3 (materiality
computation), Chapter 11 (ITGC scoping detail), Chapter 19 (final audit committee communications).

**Key continuing-case anchors:** loss of EGC status; new CFO; Kestrel acquisition; the FY2025 engagement
team roster; the planning-stage discussion of the Q4 bookings concentration.

**Walkthrough:** building the FY2025 audit strategy memo from the prior-year file, step by step.

**Case study:** the scoping decision on AtlasFlow Pty Ltd (Australia) — full scope, specified procedures, or
analytical procedures only.

misstatements against materiality).

**Key continuing-case anchors:** the derivation of $1,450 / $940 / $72; the UK component allocation; the
