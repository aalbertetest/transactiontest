Our Story

From a rainy bookstore meeting to a mountain garden wedding.

Maya and Theo met during a thunderstorm at Alder & Finch Books in Portland, Oregon, after both reached for the same dog-eared copy of Invisible Cities. Their first date was coffee, cinnamon rolls, and a walk that accidentally lasted four hours. Six years later, Theo proposed at sunrise on Craggy Pinnacle with their rescue dog, Olive, wearing a tiny velvet ring pouch.

All Wedding Details

The practical information

Venue

Juniper Hill Conservatory
2487 Laurel Bloom Road, Asheville, NC 28804

The ceremony, cocktail hour, dinner, and dancing are all on-site. The venue has stone paths, garden steps, and indoor restrooms.

Dress Code

Garden formal: suits, cocktail dresses, dressy jumpsuits, and elevated separates are welcome. Suggested colors are sage, navy, rose, copper, and cream.

Block heels or flats are recommended because part of the celebration is on grass and gravel.

Weather Plan

If it rains, the ceremony moves inside the glass conservatory with the same start time. Clear umbrellas and blanket baskets will be available.

Food

Dinner options: rosemary chicken, miso-glazed salmon, wild mushroom risotto, or children's pasta. All meals can be made gluten-free on request.

Please list allergies in the RSVP form by August 1.

Drinks

Signature cocktails: Maya's Blackberry Sage Spritz and Theo's Smoked Maple Old Fashioned. Beer, wine, cider, zero-proof spritzes, tea, and coffee are included.

Music

Ceremony music by Blue Ridge Strings. Reception music by DJ Mina Ray. Guest song requests can be submitted with the RSVP.

Children

Children listed on your invitation are warmly invited. A supervised kids' corner will include crafts, snacks, pillows, and a movie from 7:30 PM to 10:30 PM.

Photos

The ceremony is unplugged, so please keep phones away until cocktail hour. After that, share photos with the hashtag #MayaAndTheo2027.

Accessibility

Accessible parking is available beside the greenhouse entrance. Golf cart transport can be requested at arrival. The dinner space and restrooms are wheelchair accessible.

Gifts

Your presence is the best gift. If you would like to give something, Maya and Theo have a small honeymoon and home registry at Honeycomb Registry.

Contact

Questions before the wedding: email hello@mayaandtheo.example.

Day-of contact: coordinator Lina Patel at (828) 555-0148.

Important Notes

Please arrive before 4:15 PM, bring a light layer for the evening, and avoid parking on Laurel Bloom Road. The venue is smoke-free.

Travel & Stay

Getting to Asheville

  • Nearest airport: Asheville Regional Airport, 18 miles from downtown.
  • Hotel block: Camellia Hotel, 41 Market Street, code BENNETTALVAREZ.
  • Alternative stay: The Grove Park Inn, 290 Macon Avenue.
  • Parking: Limited on-site parking by permit only; shuttles are strongly encouraged.
  • Ride share: Available downtown, but pickup can be slow after 10:30 PM.

Shuttle Stops

Stop 1: Camellia Hotel courtyard at 2:45 PM and 3:15 PM.

Stop 2: Grove Park Inn lobby at 2:30 PM and 3:00 PM.

Return: Continuous loops from 10:15 PM to 11:45 PM.
